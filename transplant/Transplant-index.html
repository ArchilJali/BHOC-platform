How to interpret these numbers

The catalogue contains clinical, preclinical, analytical and review records. Evidence type and original identifiers should be read at publication level. A source link confirms traceability, not the strength or outcome of a study.

Records without a confirmed original identifier remain in the catalogue for further review and are not presented as validated clinical claims.

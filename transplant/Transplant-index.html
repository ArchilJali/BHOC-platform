BHOC and oxygen therapeutics in transplantation

BHOC (Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carrier) is the platform term used by this knowledge hub. The catalogue also preserves established scientific search terminology — HBOC, HBOC-201, Hemopure and Oxyglobin — so researchers can find the historical evidence under the names used in the original literature.

The transplant collection covers normothermic machine perfusion (NMP), ex-vivo organ perfusion, graft preservation, ischemia-reperfusion injury and tissue-level oxygenation. Records are organized by organ and linked to DOI or PubMed whenever an original identifier is available.

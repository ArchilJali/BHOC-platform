01 / Liver

Hypothermic Oxygenated Perfusion Versus Static Cold Storage in Liver Transplantation: A Meta-Analysis of Randomized Trials

Year:
2026
Design:
Meta-analysis of randomized trials
Modality:
HOPE / preservation

Population / data: 8 randomized trials; 984 adult liver transplant recipients

Published signal: HOPE was associated with lower early allograft dysfunction, primary non-function, major complications and biliary complications than static cold storage; one-year graft survival was comparable.

Limit: HOPE was compared with static cold storage, not with an oxygen-carrier intervention.

DOI: 10.1111/ctr.70676 ↗ · PubMed ↗

02 / Kidney

Machine Perfusion Parameters and 1-Year Outcomes in High KDPI Kidney Transplantation: A Single-Center Cohort Study

Year:
2026
Design:
Single-center retrospective cohort
Modality:
Hypothermic machine perfusion

Population / data: 71 recipients of kidneys with KDPI ≥85

Published signal: Pre-specified machine-perfusion flow and resistance groups did not show statistically significant differences in one-year renal function, graft survival or patient survival.

Limit: Observational, single-center evidence; no oxygen-carrier intervention was tested.

DOI: 10.1016/j.transproceed.2026.07.036 ↗ · PubMed ↗

03 / Lung

Early-to-Midterm Outcomes of Lung Transplantation Following Procurement with Thoracoabdominal Regional Perfusion: An OPTN Analysis

Year:
2026
Design:
OPTN retrospective analysis
Modality:
TA-NRP / procurement

Population / data: DCD lung transplantation; 1364 standard rapid recovery and 261 TA-NRP recipients

Published signal: One- and three-year survival were comparable. After adjustment, TA-NRP recipients had lower odds of ECMO use at 72 hours.

Limit: Registry analysis; no oxygen-carrier comparison.

DOI: 10.1016/j.healun.2026.09.005 ↗ · PubMed ↗

04 / Liver

Real-World Analysis of Hypothermic Oxygenated Perfusion and Normothermic Machine Perfusion in Liver Transplantation: An International Cohort Study

Year:
2026
Design:
International real-world cohort
Modality:
HOPE vs NMP

Population / data: 954 NMP-treated and 1202 HOPE-treated liver grafts

Published signal: The study compared contemporary HOPE and NMP practice across international cohorts with substantial baseline donor-risk differences and risk-adjusted analyses.

Limit: Non-randomized comparison with baseline donor-risk differences.

DOI: 10.1097/SLA.0000000000007195 ↗ · PubMed ↗

05 / Liver

Real-World Use of Normothermic Machine Perfusion in Liver Transplantation: A Multi-Institutional Experience From the National Organ Perfusion Registry

Year:
2026
Design:
Multicenter registry study
Modality:
Normothermic machine perfusion

Population / data: 545 NMP liver transplants across nine U.S. centers

Published signal: The registry described diverse NMP practice and reported a 90-day death-censored graft failure rate of 1.7%.

Limit: Descriptive registry evidence without a randomized control group.

DOI: 10.1111/ctr.70664 ↗ · PubMed ↗

06 / Kidney

Results of Two Cases of Pig-to-Human Kidney Xenotransplantation

Montgomery RA, Stern JM, Lonze BE, et al. · NEJM · 19 May 2022

Design:
Two-case human decedent study
Observation:
54 hours

Published signal: Short-term xenograft function without biopsy signs of hyperacute or antibody-mediated rejection during the observation window.

Limit: Short-term decedent study; it cannot establish outcomes in living recipients.

DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa2120238 ↗ · PubMed / full author list ↗

07 / Kidney

Physiology and immunology of a pig-to-human decedent kidney xenotransplant

Montgomery RA, Stern JM, Fathi F, Suek N, et al. · Nature · First online 13 November 2025; issue 5 February 2026

Design:
Single-recipient human decedent study
Observation:
61 days

Published signal: Dialysis independence; rejection on day 33, reported as reversed after treatment.

Limit: Rejection occurred on day 33; one decedent study cannot establish long-term outcomes in living recipients.

## 📖 About BHOC

<table>
  <tr>
    <td style="border: 2px solid #1a365d; border-radius: 10px; padding: 22px 28px; background-color: #f7fafc;">
      <strong>BHOC (Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carrier)</strong> is a hemoglobin-based oxygen carrier platform focused on supporting oxygen delivery beyond the conventional red blood cell transfusion model.
      <br><br>
      The central concept is <strong>Precision Oxygen Therapeutics</strong>: focusing on oxygen delivery at the tissue level rather than on blood hemoglobin concentration alone.
      <br><br>
      BHOC is intended as a complementary approach to the oxygen-delivery function of blood, while recognizing that blood performs many other essential physiological functions.
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>

## 🫁 Tissue-Level Oxygen Delivery

<table>
  <tr>
    <td style="border: 2px solid #1a365d; border-radius: 10px; padding: 22px 28px; background-color: #f7fafc;">
      Systemic measurements such as hemoglobin concentration and arterial oxygen saturation are important indicators of oxygen transport.
      <br><br>
      However, tissue oxygenation is influenced by multiple physiological processes, including:
      <br><br>
      <table style="border-collapse: collapse; border: none;">
        <tr>
          <td style="border: none; padding: 3px 10px;">• Oxygen content</td>
          <td style="border: none; padding: 3px 10px;">• Cardiac output</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td style="border: none; padding: 3px 10px;">• Microcirculation</td>
          <td style="border: none; padding: 3px 10px;">• Oxygen extraction</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td style="border: none; padding: 3px 10px;">• Diffusion</td>
          <td style="border: none; padding: 3px 10px;">• Tissue oxygen demand</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td style="border: none; padding: 3px 10px;">• Cellular oxygen utilization</td>
          <td style="border: none; padding: 3px 10px;"></td>
        </tr>
      </table>
      <br>
      BHOC research and development is centered on the broader question of how hemoglobin-based oxygen transport can contribute to oxygen availability at the tissue level.
    </td>

## 🩸 BHOC and Blood

<table>
  <tr>
    <td style="border: 2px solid #1a365d; border-radius: 10px; padding: 22px 28px; background-color: #f7fafc;">
      Blood performs many essential physiological functions, including:
      <br><br>
      <table style="border-collapse: collapse; border: none;">
        <tr>
          <td style="border: none; padding: 3px 10px;">• Oxygen carrier transport and distribution</td>
          <td style="border: none; padding: 3px 10px;">• Cellular and protein components critical to osmotic pressure fluid balance</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td style="border: none; padding: 3px 10px;">• Carbon dioxide transport</td>
          <td style="border: none; padding: 3px 10px;">• Coagulation</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td style="border: none; padding: 3px 10px;">• Immune system management functions</td>
          <td style="border: none; padding: 3px 10px;">• Acid-base regulation as well as ionic balance</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td style="border: none; padding: 3px 10px;">• Signaling and other biological functions</td>
          <td style="border: none; padding: 3px 10px;"></td>
        </tr>
      </table>
      <br>
      BHOC focuses specifically on the cooperative oxygen-delivery and distribution function.
      <br><br>
      <strong>Therefore, BHOCs have been developed and evaluated as an immediate and complementary oxygenation support, rather than as a substitute for all functions of blood.</strong>
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>

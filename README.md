## 🔬 About BHOC

**BHOC (Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carrier)** is a hemoglobin-based oxygen carrier platform focused on supporting oxygen delivery beyond the conventional red blood cell transfusion model.

The central concept is **Precision Oxygen Therapeutics**: focusing on oxygen delivery at the **tissue level** rather than on blood hemoglobin concentration alone.

BHOC is intended as a **complementary approach** to the oxygen-delivery function of blood, while recognizing that blood performs many other essential physiological functions.

## 🎯 Precision Oxygen Therapeutics

Precision Oxygen Therapeutics focuses on **four fundamental questions**:

| Question | Focus |
| :---: | :--- |
| **📍 WHERE** | Where is oxygen needed? |
| **⏰ WHEN** | When is additional oxygen delivery required? |
| **📊 HOW MUCH** | How much oxygen is required to support tissue demand? |
| **🎛️ CONTROL** | How can oxygen delivery be supported in a controlled and continuous manner? |

The objective is to move the focus from simply increasing circulating hemoglobin toward understanding and supporting **oxygen availability at the tissue level**.

## 🧬 Tissue-Level Oxygen Delivery

Systemic measurements such as hemoglobin concentration and arterial oxygen saturation are important indicators of oxygen transport.

However, tissue oxygenation is influenced by **multiple physiological processes**:

| Process | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| 🫁 **Oxygen Content** | Amount of oxygen carried in the blood |
| ❤️ **Cardiac Output** | Volume of blood pumped by the heart |
| 🔬 **Microcirculation** | Blood flow through the smallest vessels |
| 📤 **Oxygen Extraction** | Oxygen removed from blood by tissues |
| 🌊 **Diffusion** | Movement of oxygen from blood to cells |
| ⚡ **Tissue Demand** | Oxygen consumption by tissues |
| 🧫 **Cellular Utilization** | Oxygen use at the mitochondrial level |

## 🩸 BHOC and Blood

Blood performs **many essential physiological functions**:

| Function | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| 🫁 **Oxygen Transport** | Carrier transport and distribution |
| 💧 **Osmotic Pressure** | Cellular and protein components for fluid balance |
| 🌬️ **CO₂ Transport** | Carbon dioxide transport |
| 🩹 **Coagulation** | Blood clotting |
| 🛡️ **Immune System** | Immune system management |
| ⚖️ **Acid-Base Balance** | Regulation and ionic balance |
| 📡 **Signaling** | Biological signaling functions |

**BHOC focuses specifically on the cooperative oxygen-delivery and distribution function.**

Therefore, BHOCs have been developed and evaluated as an **immediate and complementary oxygenation support**, rather than as a substitute for all functions of blood.

## 🧪 Scientific Principles

The development of Precision Oxygen Therapeutics requires understanding the relationship between:

```
Oxygen Supply (lung and membrane oxygenation)
        ↓
Oxygen Transport (rheology and diffusion support)
        ↓
Microvascular Distribution (perfusion and concentration)
        ↓
Tissue Oxygen Availability (extraction and control)
        ↓
Cellular Oxygen Utilization
```

> The ultimate biological objective of oxygen delivery is not simply the presence of oxygen in the circulation, but its **exchange with tissues and cells**.

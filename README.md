## 📖 About BHOC

<table>
  <tr>
    <td style="border: 2px solid #1a365d; border-radius: 10px; padding: 20px; background-color: #f7fafc;">
      <strong>BHOC (Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carrier)</strong> is a hemoglobin-based oxygen carrier platform focused on supporting oxygen delivery beyond the conventional red blood cell transfusion model.
      <br><br>
      The central concept is <strong>Precision Oxygen Therapeutics</strong>: focusing on oxygen delivery at the tissue level rather than on blood hemoglobin concentration alone.
      <br><br>
      BHOC is intended as a complementary approach to the oxygen-delivery function of blood, while recognizing that blood performs many other essential physiological functions.
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>

## 🎯 Precision Oxygen Therapeutics

Precision Oxygen Therapeutics focuses on four fundamental questions:

<table>
  <tr>
    <td align="center" style="border: 2px solid #1a365d; border-radius: 10px; padding: 20px; width: 25%; background-color: #ebf8ff;">
      <strong>📍 WHERE</strong><br><br>
      <small>Where is oxygen needed?</small>
    </td>
    <td align="center" style="border: 2px solid #1a365d; border-radius: 10px; padding: 20px; width: 25%; background-color: #e6fffa;">
      <strong>⏰ WHEN</strong><br><br>
      <small>When is additional oxygen delivery required?</small>
    </td>
    <td align="center" style="border: 2px solid #1a365d; border-radius: 10px; padding: 20px; width: 25%; background-color: #fefcbf;">
      <strong>📊 HOW MUCH</strong><br><br>
      <small>How much oxygen is required to support tissue demand?</small>
    </td>
    <td align="center" style="border: 2px solid #1a365d; border-radius: 10px; padding: 20px; width: 25%; background-color: #fed7d0;">
      <strong>🎮 CONTROL</strong><br><br>
      <small>How can oxygen delivery be supported in a controlled and continuous manner?</small>
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>

<br>

<table>
  <tr>

## 🫁 Tissue-Level Oxygen Delivery

<table>
  <tr>
    <td style="border: 2px solid #1a365d; border-radius: 10px; padding: 20px; background-color: #f7fafc;">

## 🩸 BHOC and Blood

<table>
  <tr>
    <td style="border: 2px solid #1a365d; border-radius: 10px; padding: 20px; background-color: #f7fafc;">
      Blood performs many essential physiological functions, including:
      <br><br>
      <table>
        <tr>
          <td style="border: none; padding: 5px;">• Oxygen carrier transport and distribution</td>
          <td style="border: none; padding: 5px;">• Cellular and protein components that are critical to osmotic pressure fluid balance</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td style="border: none; padding: 5px;">• Carbon dioxide transport</td>
          <td style="border: none; padding: 5px;">• Coagulation</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td style="border: none; padding: 5px;">• Immune system management functions</td>
          <td style="border: none; padding: 5px;">• Acid-base regulation as well as ionic balance</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td style="border: none; padding: 5px;">• Signaling and other biological functions</td>
          <td style="border: none; padding: 5px;"></td>
        </tr>
      </table>
      <br>
      BHOC focuses specifically on the cooperative oxygen-delivery and distribution function.
      <br><br>
      <strong>Therefore, BHOCs have been developed and evaluated as an immediate and complementary oxygenation support, rather than as a substitute for all functions of blood.</strong>
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>

## 🧬 Scientific Principles — Oxygen Pathway

<table>
  <tr>
    <td style="border: 2px solid #1a365d; border-radius: 10px; padding: 20px; background-color: #f7fafc;">
      The development of Precision Oxygen Therapeutics requires understanding the relationship between:
      <br><br>
      <code>
      <strong>Oxygen Supply</strong> (lung and membrane oxygenation and anti-oxidation control)<br>
      ↓<br>
      <strong>Oxygen Transport</strong> (rheology and diffusion support, ECMO)<br>
      ↓<br>
      <strong>Microvascular Distribution</strong> (perfusion and concentration)<br>
      ↓<br>
      <strong>Tissue Oxygen Availability</strong> (extraction and control)<br>
      ↓<br>
      <strong>Cellular Oxygen Utilization</strong>
      </code>
      <br><br>
      <strong>The ultimate biological objective of oxygen delivery is not simply the presence of oxygen in the circulation, but its exchange with tissues and cells.</strong>
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>

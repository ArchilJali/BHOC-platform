## 🔬 About BHOC

**BHOC (Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carrier)** is a hemoglobin-based oxygen carrier platform focused on supporting oxygen delivery beyond the conventional red blood cell transfusion model.

The central concept is **Precision Oxygen Therapeutics**: focusing on oxygen delivery at the tissue level rather than on blood hemoglobin concentration alone.

BHOC is intended as a **complementary approach** to the oxygen-delivery function of blood, while recognizing that blood performs many other essential physiological functions.

---

## 🎯 Precision Oxygen Therapeutics

Precision Oxygen Therapeutics focuses on four fundamental questions:

| Question | Focus |
| :--- | :--- |
| **📍 WHERE** | Where is oxygen needed? |
| **⏰ WHEN** | When is additional oxygen delivery required? |
| **📊 HOW MUCH** | How much oxygen is required to support tissue demand? |
| **🎛️ CONTROL** | How can oxygen delivery be supported in a controlled and continuous manner? |

The objective is to move the focus from simply increasing circulating hemoglobin toward understanding and supporting **oxygen availability at the tissue level**.

---

## 🧬 Tissue-Level Oxygen Delivery

Systemic measurements such as hemoglobin concentration and arterial oxygen saturation are important indicators of oxygen transport.

However, tissue oxygenation is influenced by multiple physiological processes:

- Oxygen content
- Cardiac output
- Microcirculation
- Oxygen extraction
- Diffusion
- Tissue oxygen demand
- Cellular oxygen utilization

BHOC research and development is centered on the broader question of how hemoglobin-based oxygen transport can contribute to oxygen availability at the tissue level.

# BHOC Human Use Evidence

This directory is a new, self-contained Human Use section. Existing Veterinary and Transplant sections are not modified by this build.

## Evidence boundary

Animal and relevant veterinary records are retained only when the source catalogue assigns them to a human-development direction. They are labelled **Preclinical evidence supporting human development** and are not presented as human clinical evidence.

## Catalogue

- Total included records: 184
- Preclinical evidence supporting human development: 74
- Human clinical evidence: 46
- Pending full-text classification: 30
- Reviews, clinical context & policy: 24
- In vitro & analytical evidence: 10

## Verification

Records preserve PubMed, DOI and external-source links where available. `Matched across sources` means a record was found in at least two supplied catalogues. `Single-source — review` remains pending verification against an original identifier or full text.

The catalogue maps evidence. It does not make a regulatory claim, establish clinical efficacy, or provide a clinical recommendation.

- Record 385: Effects of Dietary Modifications on Cholesterol-Induced Anemis in Guinea Pigs — Veterinary-only record without a clear BHOC/HBOC translational link.

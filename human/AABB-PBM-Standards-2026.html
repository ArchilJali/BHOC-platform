AABB Patient Blood Management Standards 2026

A source-linked review of the proposed sixth edition and its relevance to future blood and biotherapy quality systems.

Human Use evidence hub →AABB source ↗

On 11 September 2026, AABB opened public comment on the proposed sixth edition of its Standards for a Patient Blood Management Program. The comment period remains open through 10 November 2026.

What the proposed standards address

  • Monitoring critical technology infrastructure and defined checks that systems are working as intended.
  • Use, risk management, verification of output and monitoring of artificial intelligence in an accredited facility.
  • A specific informed-consent process for patients who lack decisional capacity.
  • Review of deviations, nonconformances and adverse events to identify safety trends.
The direction is clear: advanced blood and biotherapy programs need traceable technology, controlled decision-making and continuous safety review.

Relevance to Precision Oxygen Therapeutics

This proposed standard is not evidence for BHOC and does not establish any regulatory requirement for a BHOC product. It does, however, provide a useful quality-system reference for future oxygen-delivery programs.

Any future Precision Oxygen Therapeutics program will need more than a product description. It will need equipment verification, documented process controls, clinical governance, traceable records, monitoring of decision-support tools and a defined method for reviewing adverse events and deviations.

Evidence boundary: the sixth edition is still a proposed standard. It will be finalized after the public-comment period. This page reports the proposal and does not present it as a final standard, clinical evidence or BHOC approval.

Primary source:
AABB, Comment Period Opens for Proposed 6th Edition of Standards for a Patient Blood Management Program, 11 September 2026.

Cornerstone draft · veterinary oxygen carriers

Oxyglobin is the historical veterinary bridge to HBOC and BHOC.

Oxyglobin (hemoglobin glutamer-200, bovine) created a regulated veterinary precedent for hemoglobin-based oxygen-carrying support in anemic dogs. This page is designed to connect that known search and evidence territory with the broader HBOC literature and the emerging BHOC — Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carrier — framework without implying a new approval or equivalence.

Draft evidence boundary. Oxyglobin approval is not BHOC approval. Evidence in dogs does not establish use in cats, horses, wildlife or other species. Off-label publications must be labelled as such. Historical terms such as “blood substitute” may be discussed for search/history, but the visible scientific framing should state that hemoglobin oxygen carriers address oxygen-carrying support and do not reproduce all functions of donor blood.

Veterinary applications · staging concept

Blood is a system.
Oxygen delivery is a function.

Veterinary transfusion medicine begins with the patient in front of us: severe anemia, hemorrhage, surgery, donor-blood availability and the time needed to restore oxygen-carrying capacity. This VET direction connects those clinical problems with source-linked evidence on Oxyglobin, veterinary HBOCs and the emerging BHOC framework.

Evidence boundary. Oxyglobin regulatory approvals concern dogs and do not establish approval for other species or for a new BHOC formulation. Published off-label or translational evidence must remain labelled by species and study type. BHOC is presented here as an emerging research/development category, not as a currently approved veterinary treatment. Donor blood remains a multi-function biological therapy; oxygen-carrier research addresses the oxygen-delivery function rather than reproducing all blood functions.

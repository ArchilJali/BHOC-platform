Evidence boundary

Separate direct product evidence from the world around it.

Real-World Evidence is the broader evidence layer. Direct BHOC / HBOC records, regulatory history and publication libraries remain in the BHOC Therapeutics Platform. Comparator evidence, clinical-system constraints and oxygen-delivery context are kept distinct so that evidence can be traced without blending product claims with the clinical environment.

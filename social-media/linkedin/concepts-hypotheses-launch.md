Status: READY FOR PUBLICATION

We have launched a new **Concepts & Hypotheses** section on the BHOC Therapeutics Platform.

The purpose is simple: separate developing scientific ideas from established evidence.

The first three research concepts are now live:

• Are we identifying the oxygen-delivery problem early enough in prehospital care?
• Should oxygen-delivery potency matter more than hemoglobin concentration alone?
• Can the PPH treatment window be defined by oxygen-delivery risk, not blood-loss volume alone?

Each page is source-linked and clearly marked **Research Concept · Hypothesis**.

These are not clinical recommendations or peer-reviewed conclusions. They are questions we believe are worth defining and testing properly.

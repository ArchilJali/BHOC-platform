Physiology · Nitric oxide · Microcirculation

Hemoglobin βCys93, NO signaling and tissue oxygen delivery

A BHOC discussion of the 2015 PNAS βCys93 study: normal hemoglobin concentration and oxygen-carrying capacity did not prevent impaired hypoxic vasodilation and reduced tissue oxygenation when a key hemoglobin NO-signaling site was disrupted. The evidence reinforces that oxygen content, vascular regulation and microcirculatory flow must be considered together.

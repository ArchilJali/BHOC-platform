## 📄 Publications

- [The New York Times featured Biopure's Hemopure in 2001](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/archil-jaliashvili-bhoc_the-new-york-times-article-david-n-judelson-activity-7498456973898596352-Y-I0?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAAEQr9jgBTdhh4g1EK3Oh0sejDsmLfy_HLHk)
- *David N. Judelson, former President and co-founder of Gulf+Western, played a pivotal role as strategic and financial partner to Biopure CEO Carl Rausch during the company's most transformative years. In 2001, The New York Times featured Biopure Corporation for its groundbreaking development of Hemopure – an oxygen-carrying blood substitute derived from purified bovine hemoglobin. Often called the "Fifth Beatle" of Biopure, Judelson helped navigate complex regulatory, financial, and technological landscapes. Biopure remains the only company ever to bring both human and veterinary HBOCs to market. BHOC continues this legacy of innovation.*

Kennedy, this collaboration validated the technology's potential for saving lives in extreme conditions. Biopure remains the only company to bring both human and veterinary HBOCs to market. BHOC continues this mission for trauma and emergency care.*

- [Global Blood Donation Crisis and the Need for Functional Resilience](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7498260816098410496/)
- *On August 26, 2026, three major blood services – Australian Red Cross Lifeblood, Canadian Blood Services, and NHS Blood and Transplant – issued a joint call for more blood donors. This follows repeated warnings from the American Red Cross over the past two years about blood shortages, with the UK and other high-income countries also reporting declining supplies. A critical look at the global blood shortage crisis, the limits of donor recruitment, and the need for functional resilience in blood systems. Connects directly to BHOC's mission of supporting oxygen transport without donor dependency.*




- [Hemoglobin βCys93, nitric oxide signaling and tissue oxygen delivery](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/bhoc-therapeutics_hemoglobin-%CE%B2cys93-is-essential-for-cardiovascular-activity-7436875803931566080-yQDt?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAAEQr9jgBTdhh4g1EK3Oh0sejDsmLfy_HLHk)
- *BHOC discussion of the 2015 PNAS study on the conserved hemoglobin βCys93 residue, S-nitrosothiol/NO bioactivity, hypoxic vasodilation and tissue oxygenation. The study showed that disrupting βCys93 in mice impaired blood-flow autoregulation and tissue oxygenation despite preserved hemoglobin oxygen-carrying capacity. The BHOC relevance is the broader physiology: effective oxygen delivery depends on oxygen carriage, vascular signaling and microcirculatory flow, while NO interactions of cell-free hemoglobin oxygen carriers require technology-specific evaluation.*

Possible research directions

Areas for scientific discussion include management of cancer-associated anemia, tissue hypoxia, perioperative oxygen delivery, suitable physiological endpoints and research partnerships. These are potential study topics, not established indications.

Evidence classification: the 2009 study belongs to the historical Oxyglobin product catalogue. The 2013 and 2015 oncology papers belong to Related Scientific Evidence and are not counted as Oxyglobin efficacy studies.

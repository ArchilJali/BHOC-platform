Severe acute inflammation, microcirculation and tissue oxygenation

Nimmo A, Younsi A. Oxygen. 2026;6(3):22.

Reviews how severe inflammation, endothelial dysfunction, edema and impaired microcirculation can disrupt tissue oxygenation, providing pathophysiological context for Precision Oxygenation Therapeutics and BHOC/HBOC research.

Defining and quantifying oxygen delivery potency of blood products

Rogers SC, Brummet M, Tobin KV, et al. Blood Red Cells & Iron. 2026;2(2):100054.

Introduces the L-TOF framework for comparing oxygen-delivery potency and examines how storage age changes the volume of red-cell products required to match fresh blood.

Nitric oxide scavenging, vasoconstriction and tissue oxygenation

Zhang R, Hess DT, Qian Z, et al. PNAS. 2015;112(20):6425–6430.

Shows why oxygen-carrying capacity alone cannot define effective oxygen delivery: hemoglobin-linked NO bioactivity, hypoxic vasodilation and microcirculatory blood flow are part of the tissue-oxygenation equation.

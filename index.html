Precision Oxygenation Therapeutics

From oxygen in circulation to oxygen where it is needed.

Hemoglobin concentration and arterial saturation matter, but tissue oxygenation also depends on flow, microvascular distribution, diffusion, extraction and cellular utilisation. BHOC focuses on the oxygen-delivery function while recognising that blood performs many other essential functions.

The foundation and the future

Built on decades of work. Carried forward with purpose.

This platform stands on a vast scientific, clinical, regulatory and engineering foundation created by researchers, clinicians, institutions, regulators, manufacturing teams and development partners around the world. We recognise and thank every contributor who advanced this field at every stage of its evolution.

The core innovation team remains intact. In the hands of innovators, innovation does not stand still; it evolves, improves and moves forward.

BHOC and Precision Oxygenation Therapeutics are presented as a scientific and technology-development framework. Historical product names and HBOC terminology are retained for evidence discovery. Publication inclusion does not establish regulatory approval, efficacy or a clinical recommendation.

## Scientific collaboration questions

These publications justify a focused discussion with Dr. Zambelli about unmet clinical needs in veterinary oncology and potential investigator-led oxygenation studies. Research questions include the relationship between tissue hypoxia and treatment responses, management of anemia and perioperative oxygen delivery, suitable outcome measurements, possible university or clinical R&D partners, and translational development opportunities.

**Evidence boundary:** No claim is made that BHOC alters tumor biology, improves oncologic treatment outcomes, or is approved for cancer treatment. Any oncology application would require dedicated preclinical and clinical investigation.

Oxyglobin / HBOC-301 — regulatory approval, clinical efficacy, oxygen delivery & scientific evidence.

FDA pivotal efficacy

95% vs 32% treatment success in the efficacy population.

The original FDA field trial enrolled 64 client-owned anaemic dogs: 30 randomized to Oxyglobin and 34 to untreated control. Treatment success meant no additional oxygen-carrying support during the following 24 hours; it was not a survival endpoint.

Efficacy population: 20/21 (95%) Oxyglobin vs 9/28 (32%) control, p≤0.001. Intent-to-treat: 22/30 (73%) vs 10/34 (29%), p≤0.001.

EMA cause-stratified efficacy

100% treatment success in two evaluable anaemia strata.

The EMA scientific discussion reports treatment success by cause of anaemia using the same 24-hour efficacy concept: no additional oxygen-carrying support was required during the efficacy window.

Prospective randomized clinical comparison · 2009

Oxyglobin vs packed red blood cells in canine babesiosis.

Zambelli AB, Leisewitz AL. Twelve dogs with naturally occurring Babesia rossi anaemia were randomized to Oxyglobin or packed red blood cell transfusion. The study reported similar overall improvements in blood-gas, acid-base and blood-pressure measures, while packed cells tended to normalize habitus and appetite more rapidly.

PMID 19691591 · DOI 10.1111/j.1476-4431.2009.00386.x

How this index was built

  • Each ID points to one printed form and its source-page position; it does not assert a unique patient.
  • Clinician, clinic, city, patient name and the original free-text comment are not republished.
  • “How given” means the sequence reported on the form. The individual case forms do not state an administration route, so route is shown as not stated.
  • Bag count and dose are transcribed separately. A missing dose is never calculated from weight or bag count.
  • Deaths, euthanasia, poor response and later adverse outcomes are retained when explicitly reported.

Source: Oxyglobin Solution - Case Listing Book II, Biopure Corporation, printed October 2000. For interpretation and limitations, return to the critical archive review.

## 🔑 Key Takeaways

### ✅ What Bridge Evidence Shows
- **Oxyglobin provides critical time** — allowing veterinary teams to:
  - Find or obtain compatible blood
  - Stabilize patients for surgery
  - Transport to advanced care facilities
  - Allow medications (steroids, immunosuppressants) to take effect
  - Give owners time to make treatment decisions

### ⚠️ Important Caveats
- Most cases are **anecdotal, uncontrolled, and company-collected**
- Many cases involve **combined treatments** (blood, fluids, medications)
- Clinician-reported outcomes are **subjective**
- Bridge evidence is **supportive, not definitive proof of efficacy**

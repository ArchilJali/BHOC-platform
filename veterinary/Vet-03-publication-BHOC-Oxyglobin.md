## Evidence boundary

**Publications** document the scientific literature. **Regulatory records** document specific Oxyglobin approvals and product information. **VET Applications** organize clinical needs and species routes. These functions link to one another but are not duplicated.

Historical Oxyglobin evidence is kept distinct from future BHOC development.

Oxyglobin: the veterinary evidence bridge from HBOC history to BHOC.

Oxyglobin (hemoglobin glutamer-200, bovine) established a regulated veterinary precedent for hemoglobin-based oxygen-carrying support in anemic dogs. This page connects that known veterinary term and evidence base with HBOC terminology and the developing BHOC — Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carrier — framework, without implying a new approval or product equivalence.

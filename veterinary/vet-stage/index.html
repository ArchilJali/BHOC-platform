BHOC VET mission · biodiversity

RED BOOK Mission.
Protecting life means protecting diversity.

Veterinary oxygen-delivery science should not stop at companion animals. Wildlife rescue, zoo medicine and the care of rare or threatened species face the same time-critical problem: compatible donor blood may be difficult or impossible to obtain when tissue oxygen delivery is failing.

This future direction will connect responsibly labelled evidence across wildlife, avian, camelid and aquatic medicine. It is a scientific and humanitarian mission — not an approved-use claim.

Evidence boundary: Oxyglobin regulatory approvals concern dogs and do not establish approval for other species or for a new BHOC formulation. Published off-label and translational evidence must remain labelled. BHOC is presented as a developing research/category framework, not a currently approved veterinary treatment. Donor blood performs many biological functions; oxygen-carrier research addresses oxygen-delivery support rather than replacing all blood functions.

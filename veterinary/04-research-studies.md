## 🔑 Key Takeaways

1. **Experimental evidence (LIT-002–LIT-012):** Oxyglobin improves tissue oxygenation and microcirculation in hypovolemia models, but low-volume resuscitation alone is insufficient — adequate volume resuscitation is still required.

2. **Clinical evidence (LIT-015–LIT-017):** Positive outcomes in GDV, babesiosis, and feline anemia; however, cats with cardiac disease have high risk of circulatory overload.

3. **Analytical validation (LIT-006–LIT-008, LIT-020–LIT-023):** Oxyglobin can interfere with某些 laboratory measurements (e.g., oxygen saturation, lactate); validated analyzers should be used.

4. **Safety caveat (LIT-017):** In cats, 24-hour survival was 77%, but cardiac disease was associated with life-threatening circulatory overload.

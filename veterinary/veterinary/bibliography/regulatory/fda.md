## 🔑 Key Takeaways

1. **Approval:** FDA-approved since 1998 (NADA 141-067) for canine anemia; EMA approved in 1999.
2. **Dosing:** Original 30 mL/kg replaced with flexible 10–30 mL/kg (2006).
3. **Safety:** Primary risk is volume overload at >10 mL/kg/hr.
4. **Comparability:** Oxyglobin and HBOC-201 are nearly identical in O₂-binding characteristics.
5. **Efficacy:** 95% success in efficacy population; 73% in intent-to-treat.

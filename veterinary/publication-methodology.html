Citation coverage

The original 236-record bibliography is retained. 235 citations have identified original records: 225 PubMed citations and 10 additional journal / DOI records. 217 have a DOI. A DOI-only record is not presented as a PubMed record.

The initial pass compared titles, first authors and years against MED records in Europe PMC. The continuation reviewed shortened titles, historical journal articles and publisher records. Original supplied titles and authors are retained; source titles and authors are separately available. Publication year means the original issue year, not a later archive upload date.

Author institutions

219 records have explicit author-affiliation evidence, covering 154 institution labels. These come from indexed affiliations or article-level publisher information; any separately consulted affiliation source is linked in the result. Original source wording and spelling aliases are retained. No assignment is made from a current author profile, an email address or a private address.

Institutions can be universities, hospitals, companies, laboratories or research agencies. One publication may appear under several. Older indexing often records only the first author’s institution, so this is not a complete list of participants or an endorsement of BHOC.

Confirmed study locations

This is a separate, partial review: 1 record currently has explicit study-location evidence. The directory does not assume that work took place at every author’s institution. Missing site information means not independently established in this index, not that the study had no location.

For Potgieter and James’s four clinical cases, the journal title and abstract identify Groote Schuur Hospital, Cape Town. The authors’ University of Cape Town affiliation is recorded separately. Open the confirmed-location selection →.

Species and models

Species labels use indexed MeSH headings and explicit title words, with source-backed manual checks where stated. They are discovery labels, not a complete study-design assessment. A paper can appear in several groups. The exotic group combines ferret, rabbit and other-animal labels; Other / In vitro includes unlabelled records. Guinea pigs are classified as rodents; four false Porcine tags caused by a previous text-matching rule were removed.

Some labels describe sample material rather than the treated subject. For example, a canine-plasma assay can carry both Canine and In vitro. Human research and reviews provide wider context and are not veterinary approvals.

Journals and authors

Journal metrics count distinct stored labels; full names and abbreviations have not been merged. Author suggestions reproduce names in article metadata and do not resolve every spelling variant or identify distinct people. Author, institution, species, year and confirmed-location filters can be combined.

Remaining gaps

1 original citation remains unresolved: Giger and colleagues’ 1991 abstract. Reference 86 of Day’s 2003 review cites it, but that secondary reference is not passed off as the original. Supplied author spellings differ; no institution or DOI is inferred.

Evidence limits

This bibliography includes original studies, reviews, letters, case reports and background literature. It is not a systematic review, an efficacy assessment, or proof that every entry directly tests Oxyglobin or HBOC-201. Historical approvals apply only to the named product, jurisdiction and indication.

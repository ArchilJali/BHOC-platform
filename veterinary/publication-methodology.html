How publication links, institutions and species labels are recorded.

Citation matching

The existing 236-record bibliography was preserved. Titles, first-author names and publication years were compared with MED/PubMed records from Europe PMC. Near-exact matches required agreement on author and year; seven additional matches were reviewed for shortened titles, online versus print dates, or spelling errors.

222 citations have indexed PubMed sources; 204 also have a DOI. The remaining citations retain their original information and a clearly labelled search link. Matching a citation is not a scientific quality assessment.

Author institutions

209 records have institutions extracted from indexed author affiliations, covering 150 institution labels. Common spelling variants are normalised using an explicit alias list. No affiliation is inferred from an author’s name or current employer. Original affiliation text and its source are available under each result.

Many older PubMed records list only the first author’s affiliation. Therefore the directory is not a complete inventory of participating institutions or trial sites. Universities, hospitals, companies, laboratories and government research organisations may all appear. A paper can count under more than one institution.

27 records appear under “Other / institution not identified”, always the final option. This includes unmatched citations and indexed records without a usable institutional affiliation. Institutions are not presented as BHOC partners or endorsers.

Species, years and publication types

Species labels use indexed MeSH terms and explicit species or model words in the title. Labels may be incomplete; “Not specified” means there is no explicit label, not that no animal or human study was involved. Year choices are derived from the entire dataset. When the indexed publication year differs from the supplied year, both are retained in the metadata.

Evidence scope

The bibliography includes original studies, reviews, case reports, letters and background literature. It is not a systematic review. Some entries are background biology or veterinary literature rather than direct tests of Oxyglobin or HBOC-201. Inclusion does not establish efficacy, safety, regulatory approval or applicability to a new BHOC formulation.

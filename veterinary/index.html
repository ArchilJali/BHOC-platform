📚 About This Database

This database compiles 200+ peer-reviewed publications on hemoglobin-based oxygen carriers (HBOCs), with a special focus on Oxyglobin (hemoglobin glutamer-200, bovine) — the only FDA & EMA-approved HBOC for veterinary use.

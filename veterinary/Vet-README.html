Oxyglobin — The Only FDA-Approved HBOC for Veterinary Use

Welcome to the Veterinary HBOC Database — the most comprehensive collection of peer-reviewed publications, regulatory documents, and scientific resources on Oxyglobin (hemoglobin glutamer-200, bovine) and hemoglobin-based oxygen carriers (HBOCs) in veterinary medicine.

🩸 About Oxyglobin

Oxyglobin is a polymerized bovine hemoglobin-based oxygen carrier developed by Biopure Corporation. It received FDA approval in 1998 as the first and only hemoglobin-based oxygen carrier for veterinary use.

📖 About This Database

This database compiles 200+ peer-reviewed publications on hemoglobin-based oxygen carriers (HBOCs), with a special focus on Oxyglobin — the only FDA-approved HBOC for veterinary use.

Coverage Includes

  • Veterinary applications (dogs, cats, horses, exotic species)
  • Human clinical trials and surgical applications
  • Safety & toxicology (adverse effects, vasoconstriction, renal toxicity)
  • Mechanisms (oxygen delivery, nitric oxide scavenging, microcirculation)
  • Comparative studies (HBOC vs. blood transfusion vs. colloids)
  • Special populations (Jehovah's Witnesses, sickle cell crisis, trauma)
  • Transplantation (normothermic machine perfusion, organ preservation)

🧬 Scientific Concept — Precision Oxygen Therapeutics

The central concept is Precision Oxygen Therapeutics: focusing on oxygen delivery at the tissue level rather than on blood hemoglobin concentration alone.

BHOC is intended as a complementary approach to the oxygen-delivery function of blood, while recognizing that blood performs many other essential physiological functions.
